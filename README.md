# Create: Alconautics

*Copper pipes, rising steam, and a little liquid courage.*

Turn your harvest into something stronger. **Create: Alconautics** brings distillation to Create and **[Let's Do] Brewery - Farm&Charm Compat**, from mixing your first batch of mash to building an automated distillery.

## Build Your Distillery

Start with a Create Fluid Tank and a Blaze Burner. Fit a Distillation Head, connect your pipes, and guide the vapour into a cooled condenser.

Keep your apparatus supplied, watch its gauges, and remember the whistle - pressure has to go somewhere.

## A Handful of Herbs

Add a Dry Chamber beneath the vapour pipe and enhance your drinks with herbs from Farm & Charm:

- **Ribwort** extends effect duration.
- **Nettle** increases effect strength.

## Bottle Your Creations

Produce **sugar, fruit, vegetable, and grain moonshine**. Grow taller, shrink smaller, or enjoy **High Spirits**, combining strength, faster mining, and absorption.

Make grain mash from wheat, oats, or barley from Farm & Charm.

Drink your creations or display them in your tavern. Place two bottles together, mix different varieties, and check their contents just by looking at them.

Need a hand getting started? Built-in **Ponder scenes** explain the process.

## Optional Compatibility

### Sable

Physical moonshine bottles can shatter on impact with a small explosion and a cloud of intoxicating vapour.

### Create: Diesel Generators

Use moonshine as fuel for the Chemical Sprayer.

### Just Enough Items

Browse distillation and condensation in JEI, with machinery previews, fluid inputs and outputs, herbs, heating, and cooling requirements.

All integrations are optional - none of these mods is required to play Alconautics.

## Requirements

**Minecraft 1.21.1 - NeoForge**

Required mods:

- Create
- [Let's Do] Brewery - Farm&Charm Compat
- [Let's Do] Farm & Charm
